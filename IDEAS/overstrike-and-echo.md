# Overstrike and Echo (Multishot Equivalent)

*Batch: 2026-09-23 design and mechanics expansion — §4 Retrofitting spatial mechanics (The Tower).*

Replaces physical projectiles with probability-based spike damage. Overstrike clones passive ticks; Echo clones manual taps. Essential for shattering late-game progression walls.

## Fit today

- **Overlaps crits.** Attack already has Crit Chance, Crit Damage and Crit Chain, which are probability-based spike damage. A clone that repeats a hit is mathematically close to a crit with a ×2 multiplier; the difference has to be felt, not just numeric.
- Splitting tap and passive spikes is the genuinely new part, and gives tap builds and idle builds each their own scaling stat.
- Rolls must come from the saved run RNG so a resumed run stays identical (D006).
- Would be two new Attack rows, which D020's category balancing by Coin cost has to absorb.
- D009: The Tower is a shape reference; the multishot parallel is fine as inspiration, not as a coefficient source.
