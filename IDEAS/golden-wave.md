# The Golden Wave (Ultimate Weapons)

*Batch: 2026-09-23 design and mechanics expansion — §4 Retrofitting spatial mechanics (The Tower).*

A rare wave anomaly. While standing, no damage converts to Number; instead, all damage generates permanent Coins or Gems. Reverses player psychology, encouraging them to cripple their run's longevity for a massive payout of permanent wealth.

## Fit today

- A strong decision moment: run safety against permanent wealth, which is pillar 2's kind of choice.
- **Coin and Gem rates are tuned.** Coins come from waves (D010, D021) and Gems from bosses and milestones (D030). A burst source needs to fit those budgets or it becomes the dominant way to farm.
- "Rare" needs the seeded RNG (D006), or a deterministic schedule.
- The title calls it "Ultimate Weapons" but it's a wave anomaly, not an Ultimate; it would sit with encounter patterns in `src/tax_encounter.gd`, not the Ultimates category.
