# Damage Colour Palette

*Batch: 2026-09-23 design and mechanics expansion — §2 Visual feedback and game feel.*

As numbers scale exponentially, the visual interface must translate chaos into readable, satisfying semiotics.

- Grey/Off-White: Standard passive ticks.
- Yellow: Standard manual tap.
- Deep Orange: Critical Hit.
- Teal: "Echo" (Tap damage spike).
- Electric Blue: "Overstrike" (Passive damage spike).
- Neon Green: Siphon conversion.

## Fit today

- **Clashes with a written motion rule.** `docs/MOTION_SYSTEM.md` rule 2: "Severity is duration and size, never a new hue… Extra colours read as noise on this HUD." D018 also keeps the HUD to one accent. Adopting this means changing that rule on purpose.
- Two of the six colours are for mechanics that don't exist yet (Echo, Overstrike — see `overstrike-and-echo.md`).
- Crits exist today (Crit Chance, Crit Damage, Crit Chain), so passive/tap/crit/Siphon could be distinguished now.
- Colour-only distinctions need a second cue (size, shape, motion) for colour-blind players.
