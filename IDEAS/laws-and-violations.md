# Laws and Violations

*Batch: 2026-09-23 design and mechanics expansion — §7 Future systems integration.*

- **Laws (global modifiers):** Late-game rules like "Law of Attrition" (Rig upgrades degrade every 10 waves) or "Law of Gravity" (Siphon drops 1% per second).
- **Violations (endgame prestiges):** Break the laws. "Violation of the Boundary" permanently extends the 15-second timer to 16 seconds. "Violation of the Void" allows survival of a lethal hit at exactly 1 Number with a 5-second invulnerability window.

## Fit today

- Laws and Violations are already reserved names: the vision says they arrive as graph content through the modifier pipeline (pillar 5, D005), not special cases. This gives them their first concrete content.
- "Violation of the Void" overlaps Second Wind (D020), which already survives one lethal hit, leaving a share of peak Number.
- The vision doesn't yet say what a Law is for the player (a tier condition? an opt-in challenge for better rewards?) or how Violations are earned; that framing is the missing decision.
- "Permanently extends the timer" is permanent progression, so it must be saved as such and survive resets (D008).
