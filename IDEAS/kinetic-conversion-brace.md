# Kinetic Conversion (Brace Evolution)

*Batch: 2026-09-23 design and mechanics expansion — §3 The unified economy and viable meta-strategies.*

To support Defense builds, spending Number on Brace converts a percentage of the burned currency into a permanent run multiplier for passive income. The Brace button transforms from a panic shield into an aggressive investment vehicle.

## Fit today

- Brace costs 30% of Number today, bought down to a 15% floor by Brace Cost (D020), and is the Defense tab's first Rig row (D015).
- **"Permanent run multiplier" needs pinning down.** If it means "for the rest of this run", it fits law 4. If it means across runs, it breaks the Number-only-in-a-run boundary.
- Turns Brace into a spend that pays back, so the classic exploit is bracing when you don't need to. Needs a limit (only when a hit would land, diminishing returns, or a cap).
- Economy change: high risk, enters through the modifier pipeline, and needs a new run-scoped field in the active-run save block.
