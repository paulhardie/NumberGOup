# Rig Salvage (Free Upgrades)

*Batch: 2026-09-23 design and mechanics expansion — §4 Retrofitting spatial mechanics (The Tower).*

A Workshop utility stat granting a percentage chance to gain a free Rig rank upon clearing a wave. Injects variance and gives mathematically doomed runs a chance at survival.

## Fit today

- **Conflicts with how D015 set up Free Upgrade.** D015 made Free Upgrade "every Nth purchase rather than a roll" so seed reproducibility (D006) is untouched, and the Rig "opens no new RNG stream". A percentage roll reverses that.
- A deterministic version keeps the spirit: every Nth wave cleared grants a free rank, with the Workshop stat shortening N.
- Needs a rule for which row the free rank goes to (cheapest, last bought, player's pick).
